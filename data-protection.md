# Data protection rules (R10)

Owner: Deandre
The system must not be usable to target specific individuals.
Every page should follow these rules.

## Rules
1. Workload is visible only to the person and their team's manager.
2. Insights shows no names and hides any group under 3 people.
   Filters must not let you remove one person at a time.
3. Registration shows a privacy notice: what's stored, who sees it, why, and the right to see and correct it.
4. An audit log records who views whose data and when.
   Admin sees it, and unusual access is flagged.
5. Login errors never reveal whether an account exists.
   Accounts lock briefly after repeated failed attempts.
6. A "My data" page lets users see what's held about them and request corrections.
7. No rankings or leader boards of individuals.
8. Only collect what's needed: name, work email, password.
